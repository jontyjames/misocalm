# MisoCalm Marketing Copy

Lines, angles, and ideas to use across landing pages, app store listings, social content, and anywhere MisoCalm is positioned. Add to this as good lines come up.

---

## Research-Backed Lines

**Multisensory**
"Misophonia is a multisensory condition. Your tools should be multisensory too."
*(Backed by 2024 multisensory research showing positive visual context significantly reduces the unpleasantness and bodily sensations of trigger sounds, with people with misophonia experiencing the largest reductions.)*

---

## Feature Ideas (from research)

**Audio integrations** - Nature sounds, brown noise, calming soundscapes within the app. Research shows nature sounds reduce cortisol by 21% (2024 meta-analysis) and have the greatest effect on the autonomic nervous system (Buxton et al., 2021, PNAS). Could complement the existing visual environment to make the app a full multisensory regulation tool.
